How it works

  • The list button opens straight into your lists. His face moves into the top-right corner and stays live there; tap it to go back to him.
  • Inside a list: back and the list name on the left; edit, remove and play beside his face on the right.
  • Play walks the list one item at a time, read aloud. Answer done, skip or back.
  • Ticked items move under Done, so a daily checklist can be reused with Untick all.
  • Removing a list always asks first.

Same character, same logic

  • The face is the approved build's code, unchanged: palette, geometry, expressions, lip sync.
  • Every line you say goes through the same sentence reader as the app: expression, then low / mid / intense, and paragraphs play as beats.
  • List events add small reactions on top: a low happy on a tick, delight when a list is finished.
  • Planned in code: lists and list_items tables in store.py, and brain tools create_list, add_list_item, set_item_done, show_list, play_list.
