The nine states, in reading order. EVERY state must change BOTH the eye shape AND
the mouth stroke — never let a state be identified by the mouth alone:

1. AT REST. Eyes at their default shape and size, pupils centred and looking straight
at the viewer. Mouth a short, level, neutral stroke.

2. LISTENING. Eyes noticeably taller and more open than at rest, pupils large and
locked on the viewer, the whole face lifted slightly. Mouth a short level stroke,
very slightly wider than at rest.

3. THINKING. Pupils rolled clearly UP and off to one side, away from the viewer, the
eyes slightly compressed along the bottom. Mouth a small short stroke pushed a little
off centre.

4. SEARCHING. Eyes squashed into wide, short, narrow horizontal shapes, focused, with
the pupils pushed hard to one side mid-scan. Mouth reduced to a tiny short dash.

5. ANSWERING. Eyes relaxed and fully open, softer than listening, pupils centred on
the viewer. Mouth a gentle shallow UPWARD curve.

6. DELIGHT. Eyes squeezed into two strong UPWARD-curving crescents, the pupils hidden
by the squeeze, the brightest glow of the nine. Mouth a clear wide upward curve.

7. CONFUSION. Deliberately ASYMMETRIC: one eye noticeably larger and rounder than the
other, both pupils pushed to the same side, the whole face tilted very slightly.
Mouth a small off-centre stroke with a slight wave in it.

8. APOLOGETIC. Eyes half closed from ABOVE, the upper edge of each eye sloping DOWN
from the outer corner toward the inner corner, pupils cast downward. Mouth a small
shallow DOWNWARD curve. Dimmer than the others. Sad and sorry, never angry — the
upper edge must not slope the other way.

9. ASLEEP. Eyes fully closed, each reduced to a soft shallow horizontal curve with no
pupil visible at all. Mouth a very small level stroke. The dimmest glow of the nine.
