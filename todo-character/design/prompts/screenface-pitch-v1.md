A character design pitch sheet showing SIX different face concepts for a small home
assistant device. Clean pure-white background, a 3 by 2 grid, six panels, even
generous spacing, identical panel size and identical framing in every cell. No text,
no labels, no numbers, no captions, no borders around the grid.

Each of the six panels shows THE SAME piece of hardware: a small rounded-corner
display screen, seen straight on, face-on to the viewer, filling most of its panel.
The screen is dark near-black glass with a very subtle soft reflection and a slim
neutral light-grey bezel. The six panels differ ONLY in the face glowing on the
screen. Each face is pure emissive light on the dark glass, with a soft bloom around
the glowing shapes. Every face is centred and friendly, in a calm neutral resting
expression.

The six faces, in reading order:

1. TOP LEFT — Two tall rounded-rectangle eyes, like softened capsules standing
upright, glowing warm amber. Nothing else on the screen. Extremely minimal and
geometric.

2. TOP CENTRE — Two large round cartoon eyes with a soft cream-white eyeball, a big
dark pupil and one bright catchlight in each, plus a gentle curved lower eyelid
cutting across the bottom of each eye. Warm and cute, like a friendly pet.

3. TOP RIGHT — One single large circular eye centred on the screen, built as
concentric rings like a camera aperture, glowing calm soft blue, with a small bright
centre. A single calm focused lens.

4. BOTTOM LEFT — A face drawn as one continuous thin elegant line stroke in warm
white, forming two simple oval eyes and a single soft curved brow line arcing above
them. Delicate, minimal, like a quick ink drawing.

5. BOTTOM CENTRE — Two simple upward-curving arcs, like a pair of smiling closed
eyes, glowing soft mint green. Nothing else. The simplest possible happy face, just
two strokes.

6. BOTTOM RIGHT — Two eyes built from a grid of small discrete glowing square dots,
like an LED dot-matrix display, in warm orange. The individual pixels are clearly
visible. Retro electronic display feel.

RENDERING: clean modern product photography of the device screens, soft even studio
lighting, slight realistic glass sheen, crisp and graphic. The glowing faces are
bright and clearly readable against the dark screens.

NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logos, no body, no limbs,
no arms, no legs, no character body of any kind outside the screen, no robot chassis,
no stand, no buttons, no cables, no background scenery, no people, no hands, no
mouths other than where described, no speech bubbles, no emoji, no sparkles, no
motion lines.
