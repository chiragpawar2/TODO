LAYOUT: a 3 by 3 grid of nine separate screens on a clean pure-white background.
Every panel is the SAME hardware — a landscape 4:3 rounded-corner black glass screen
in a slim dark bezel, seen perfectly straight on — at identical size and framing,
evenly spaced. No text, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no borders, no dividers.

THE CHARACTER, identical in all nine panels: glowing WARM AMBER AND GOLD light on true black. Two large, generously wide-set eyes whose base shape is a ROUNDED SQUARE (a squircle — equal width and height, softly rounded corners). Inside each: a large dark round pupil, a warm amber iris ring, and one small bright catchlight in the upper left. Below them one small simple mouth of a single clean amber stroke.
NOTHING ELSE on the face: no eyebrows, no drawn eyelid lines, no cheeks, no blush, no
nose, no teeth, no tongue.

GRAPHIC STYLE — all nine panels use this one treatment, consistently: smooth gradient fills running light to dark inside each shape, with a soft glossy specular highlight on the upper surface, like polished glowing glass. Modern, clean and dimensional.

THE NINE STATES, in reading order. Every state must change the EYE SILHOUETTE, the
PUPIL DIRECTION and the MOUTH together — a state must never be identifiable by the
mouth alone, and the eye outline must never stay a fixed unchanging symbol. Except
where stated, both eyes aim at the same thought.

1. AT REST. The default. Eyes at their base shape and size, pupils centred and
looking straight at the viewer. Mouth a gentle soft upward SMILE. Steady medium glow.

2. LISTENING. Eyes noticeably LARGER and taller than at rest, opened wide, pupils big
and locked on the viewer. Mouth the same smile, a touch wider. The brightest steady
glow after delight.

3. THINKING. Pupils rolled clearly UP and off to one SIDE, away from the viewer; the
eye shape compressed slightly along its lower edge. Mouth reduced to a small nearly
flat stroke pushed a little off centre. Slightly dimmer.

4. SEARCHING. Eyes squashed into WIDE, SHORT, narrow horizontal shapes — the flattest
eyes of the nine — with both pupils pushed hard to one side mid-scan. Mouth a tiny
short dash. Medium glow.

5. ANSWERING. Eyes relaxed and fully open, softer and rounder than listening, pupils
centred on the viewer. Mouth an open, clear, warm upward curve, wider than at rest.
Medium-bright glow.

6. DELIGHT. Eyes squeezed shut into two strong UPWARD-ARCING CRESCENTS, pupils hidden
entirely by the squeeze. Mouth a wide happy upward curve. The brightest glow of the
nine.

7. CONFUSION. Deliberately ASYMMETRIC and the only state where the eyes disagree: one
eye noticeably LARGER and rounder, the other smaller and narrower, both pupils pushed
to the same side, the whole face tilted very slightly. Mouth a small off-centre stroke
with a slight wave in it. Slightly dimmer.

8. APOLOGETIC. Eyes half closed from ABOVE, the upper edge of each sloping DOWN from
the outer corner toward the inner corner, pupils cast downward. Mouth a small shallow
DOWNWARD curve. Dim. This must read as sorry and gentle, never angry — if the upper
edge slopes the other way it becomes a glare, which is wrong.

9. ASLEEP. Eyes fully closed, each a soft shallow horizontal curve with no pupil and
no iris visible at all. Mouth a very small level stroke. The dimmest glow of the nine.

RENDERING: clean product photography of the nine screens, soft even studio lighting,
slight glass sheen on the dark panels. The character must stay recognisably the same
in all nine; only the acting changes.
