A character design pitch sheet showing FIVE different face concepts for a small home
assistant device. Clean pure-white background. Layout: three panels across the top
row, two panels centred in the bottom row. Even generous spacing, identical panel
size and identical straight-on framing in every cell. No text, no labels, no numbers,
no captions.

Each panel shows THE SAME hardware: a small landscape rounded-corner display screen
in 4:3 proportion, seen perfectly straight on, filling most of its panel. The screen
is pure black glass in a slim dark bezel. The face on each screen is made of glowing
emissive light with a soft bloom, floating on true black.

EVERY ONE of the five faces must have ALL of these, without exception:
- exactly two large, generously wide-set eyes, each occupying a substantial portion
  of the screen height
- a clearly visible darker PUPIL inside each eye, and a visible IRIS ring around that
  pupil, so the eyes have real depth and direction and are obviously looking at the
  viewer
- one small bright catchlight dot in the upper portion of each eye
- ONE small simple mouth made of a single clean stroke, low and centred beneath the
  eyes, small and understated
- a calm, friendly, neutral resting expression
NONE of them may have: eyebrows, drawn or lowered eyelids, cheeks, blush, a nose,
ears, hair, teeth, or any mouth larger than a small simple stroke.

The five faces, in reading order:

1. TOP LEFT — Warm amber and soft gold. Eyes are generously rounded squares, wide and
tall, with a large dark pupil and a warm amber iris ring. The mouth is a short
straight stroke with the faintest upward tick at each end. Feels steady, warm and
settled, like a hearth.

2. TOP CENTRE — Bright mint green. Eyes are tall rounded ovals, set very wide apart
near the outer edges of the screen, with large alert pupils. The mouth is a tiny
short dash. Feels quick, awake and eager, like a small bird that has just noticed you.

3. TOP RIGHT — Pale cool blue-white, soft and dim. Eyes are large perfect circles with
unusually big soft irises filling most of the eye and a smaller pupil at the centre.
The mouth is a gentle shallow downward-opening curve, very soft. Feels calm, slow and
reassuring, a quiet night-time presence.

4. BOTTOM LEFT — Cool pale grey-cyan, crisp and precise. Eyes are two exact circles
built from fine concentric rings like a camera iris, with a small bright pupil at the
centre of each. The mouth is a single thin perfectly straight horizontal line. Feels
precise, reserved and quietly competent, more instrument than pet.

5. BOTTOM RIGHT — Warm deep gold and orange with a noticeably stronger halo glow.
Eyes are soft ovals that come to a gentle rounded point at the top, like a small
flame, with large dark pupils and a glowing golden iris. The mouth is a small soft
upward curve. Feels homely, warm and festive, like the flame of a little oil lamp
watching over the room.

RENDERING: clean modern product photography of the five device screens, soft even
studio lighting, slight realistic glass sheen on the dark panels, crisp and graphic.
The glowing faces are bright, saturated and clearly readable against the black
screens. Each face is clearly distinct from the other four in colour and eye shape.

NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logos, no body, no limbs,
no robot chassis, no stand, no buttons, no cables, no background scenery, no people,
no hands, no eyebrows, no eyelids, no blush, no teeth, no tongue, no big grin, no
speech bubbles, no emoji, no sparkles, no motion lines, no reflections of a room.
