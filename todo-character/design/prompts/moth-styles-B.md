A graphic style exploration sheet for ONE character face, shown six times in six
different rendering styles.

LAYOUT: a 3 by 2 grid of six separate screens on a clean pure-white background. Every
panel shows the SAME hardware: a landscape 4:3 rounded-corner black glass screen in a
slim dark bezel, seen perfectly straight on, identical size and framing in all six.
Even generous spacing. No text, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no borders.

THE CHARACTER, identical in all six panels: a face of glowing PALE COOL BLUE-WHITE light on true black. Two large wide-set PERFECTLY CIRCULAR eyes, each with an unusually LARGE soft pale iris filling most of the eye and a smaller dark pupil at its centre, plus one small bright catchlight. Below them one small simple mouth made of a single clean short pale stroke.
The expression is the same calm neutral resting expression in all six panels, with
the pupils looking straight at the viewer. No eyebrows, no drawn eyelids, no cheeks,
no blush, no nose, no teeth.

The six graphic treatments, in reading order. The face geometry stays IDENTICAL in
all six — only the rendering style of the glowing marks changes:

1. CHUNKY PIXEL ART. Built from large chunky 8-bit blocks with hard stair-stepped
edges and no anti-aliasing. Deliberately coarse and retro-game.

2. HALFTONE DUOTONE. The shapes built from printed halftone dots that grow larger
toward the centre and smaller at the edges, like newsprint. Textured and graphic.

3. WIREFRAME TECHNICAL. Drawn as fine concentric construction rings with small tick
marks and thin crosshair guides, like a blueprint or an instrument reticle. Precise
and diagrammatic.

4. LIQUID BLOB. Soft organic rounded edges that look slightly molten, with a gentle
motion-blur trail smearing off one side of each shape, as if mid-movement.

5. HIGH CONTRAST TWO TONE. Pure flat colour with zero gradient and zero glow, extremely
bold and graphic, like a screen-printed poster. Maximum contrast against the black.

6. AIRBRUSH SOFT FOCUS. Very soft diffuse edges with no hard boundary anywhere, low
contrast, dreamy and out of focus, like an airbrushed glow.

RENDERING: clean product photography of the six screens, soft even studio lighting,
slight glass sheen on the dark panels. Each of the six must be clearly and obviously
different in treatment from the other five, while remaining the same character.

NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logos, no eyebrows, no
eyelid lines, no blush, no teeth, no nose, no body, no limbs, no hands, no stand, no
buttons, no cables, no background scenery, no people, no speech bubbles, no emoji.
