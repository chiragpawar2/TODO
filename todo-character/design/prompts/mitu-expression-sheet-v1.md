The nine poses, in reading order. Each is a DIFFERENT physical decision — do not

1. ALERT ATTENTION. Rises and tips forward as if someone just spoke to it. Weight
rolls onto the front of the feet. Eyes at their widest and fully round, pupils
straight at the viewer. Tail lifted high and held stiff and still, wing stubs pinned

2. THINKING IT OVER. The whole body tips backward and the gaze goes up and away to
one side. Weight settles onto the heels. Upper lids lowered a little over eyes that
look up and off. One wing stub lifted and curled inward, tail drooping down behind to
counterbalance the backward lean.

3. SEARCHING. Body low, long and gliding forward as if scanning along a shelf. One
foot clearly ahead of the other. Eyes narrowed to a focused horizontal shape with
pupils pushed to one side mid-scan. Tail streaming straight out behind, level with
the body.

4. ANSWERING. Settled upright and open, calm and centred, chest mass lifted slightly.
Both feet planted evenly. Eyes soft, fully open, pupils on the viewer. Both wing
stubs opened a little outward and downward in a small offering gesture, tail relaxed
and neutral.

5. DELIGHT. Caught airborne in a small hop, both feet clearly off the ground and
tucked up under the body, the body stretched slightly taller. Eyes squeezed shut into
two happy upward-curving crescents. Tail flicked sharply up, both wing stubs flared
out and up.

6. CONFUSION. The entire body tilted well over to one side, peering. Weight on one
foot with the other barely touching. The two eyes deliberately uneven — one wide open,
one half-lidded — both pupils pushed to the same side. Tail bent into a questioning
hook curve, one wing stub raised.

7. APOLOGETIC. Sinks down and compresses, visibly smaller and released, weight
dropped straight down, both feet close together and turned slightly inward. Eyes level
with lids lowered from above and pupils cast down. Both wing stubs drooping flat
against the body, tail hanging limp and low.

8. FRAZZLED. Recoils upward and stiffens, thrown off balance, feet splayed wide apart
for stability. Eyes very wide with small pupils drawn close together. Tail stiff and
sharply kinked, both wing stubs jutting straight out rigidly.

9. ASLEEP. Sunk down and settled low and wide onto its base, feet tucked out of sight
beneath the body, the softest and roundest silhouette of the nine. Eyes closed as two
gentle downward curves. Tail resting flat, wing stubs folded in close. The ring of
light at the base is dimmer than in the other poses.
