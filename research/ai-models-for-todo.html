AI models for TODO

Which brain and voice to run TODO on: options, prices, free tiers, and a recommendation. Prepared 25 November 2026. Prices change often; figures come from published pricing pages and recent third-party summaries (Sept–Nov 2026), and should be checked on each provider's own page before committing. ₹ figures use about ₹88 to the US dollar.

1. The three winners

Gemini Main brain + voice

Google. The only option with live voice in and out, tool use (lists, messages) and Marathi/Hindi/English, callable straight from the phone page.

Free tier: yes, but small and changing; resets at midnight US Pacific (1:30 pm IST).

Paid: Flash-Lite text $0.30 in / $2.50 out per million tokens; Live voice ≈ $0.005 per minute heard, $0.018 per minute spoken.

Groq Free backup

Groq (the fast-chip company, not xAI's Grok). Runs open models (GPT-OSS, Qwen) very fast, plus Whisper speech-to-text.

Free tier: generous, no card: about 30 requests a minute and 1,000 a day per model.

Role: answers typed and fallback turns when Gemini is busy, and can run typed tests for free.

Sarvam Indian voices

Sarvam AI (India). Speech-to-text (Saaras) and text-to-speech (Bulbul) built for Indian languages, including code-mixed speech.

Free: ₹100 credits for new accounts (some pages still say ₹1,000), never expire.

Paid: listening ₹0.49 a minute; speaking ₹3 per 1,000 characters (Bulbul V3) or ₹2 (V4 Flash).

Recommended setup. Gemini for conversation, lists and messages, on a paid key with a daily cap. Groq as the free backup when Gemini is busy. Sarvam as the speaking voice if Gemini's Marathi sounds off when tested on the phone. A separate Gemini key and project for testing, so tests never use the family's quota.

5. Who gives a free API key today

About ten providers still offer a real free key or lasting free allowance, with no payment needed. Three more give only trial credit.

Lasting free tier (no card)Trial credit only
Google Gemini · Groq · Mistral · OpenRouter (free models) · Cloudflare Workers AI · NVIDIA NIM (developer programme) · Cohere (trial key) · GitHub Models (not confirmed this month) · Ollama Cloud (limited sessions) · SiliconFlow (needs ID verification)Sarvam (₹100) · Cerebras ($5 for 30 days) · Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Cartesia (small trial allowances)
Catches with free tiers. Limits are small and change without notice (Gemini's has dropped twice while building TODO). Several free tiers, Gemini's included outside Europe and the UK, may use your prompts to improve their products, which matters for a family's messages. Free tiers are meant for testing, not daily use.

6. Things to act on

  • Gemini 3.1 Flash Live Preview, TODO's main voice model, is reported to have shut down on 17 November 2026; Gemini 3.8 Live replaces it. TODO's voice model needs checking and switching.
  • Older Gemini 2.5 text models are retired for new keys; TODO no longer uses them.
  • Set a daily request cap on the paid Gemini project; budget alerts alone don't stop spending.
  • Add Groq as the busy-time backup, then test Sarvam for Marathi speech.
